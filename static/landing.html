Frequently Asked Questions

Answers for Statistical Price Compilers

Unlike static physical goods, airline pricing is governed by dynamic revenue management algorithms where fares surge by 200% to 500% as departure approaches. Sampling a single ticket on the 2nd Tuesday of each month creates a +18% to +25% materiality distortion in national inflation statistics.
Airlines frequently add, cancel, and modify seasonal flight numbers. Direct chained bilateral indices drift significantly when items enter and exit the basket. GEKS computes indirect geometric paths across all periods in a rolling window, ensuring transitivity and zero chain drift.
APIx uses statutory airport fee matrices: User Development Fees (UDF: ₹180–₹380 by airport), Aviation Security Fee (ASF: statutory flat ₹200), and 5% economy GST. This isolates the true commercial price volatility from government-notified airport levies.

Automated CAPTCHA solving (2Captcha/CapSolver) is built to fulfill technical specifications but disabled by default (CAPTCHA_SOLVING_ENABLED=false) in standard public-sector deployment.
Phase 1 (Current Prototype): High-frequency scraping across 3 primary feeds (Google Flights / SerpAPI, Ixigo OTA, SpiceJet probe) over 8 DGCA-weighted domestic corridors and 5 advance booking horizons (T+1…T+45).
Phase 2 (Production Recommendation): Statutory data-sharing mandate with scheduled airlines (following TRAI telecom and US Bureau of Labor Statistics / DOT Form 41 models), with web scraping serving as an independent regulatory validation layer. See Production Readiness Roadmap.
